Fitness routines can remain simple after 40. The planning challenge often becomes fitting exercise around work, family, recovery and changing circumstances.

## Design around real life

Choose a schedule that is realistic. A plan that only works on perfect weeks will be hard to maintain.

## Keep sessions repeatable

Use a small menu of familiar workouts instead of changing the plan constantly.

## Respect recovery

Rest days and manageable intensity are part of a sustainable routine. Individual needs vary, particularly when health conditions or medications affect activity.

## Track controllable behaviors

Count sessions, walking minutes or strength days instead of relying only on outcomes that naturally fluctuate.

## Bottom line

Build the habit first and refine the program around your current life.
