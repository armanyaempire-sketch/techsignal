A marketing funnel describes how a person moves from discovering a problem to considering solutions and finally taking an action.

## Discovery

The reader may start with a broad question. The job of the page is to answer it clearly and earn enough trust for another useful step.

## Evaluation

The reader now wants decision criteria. This is where comparisons, pricing context, features and common mistakes become valuable.

## Decision

A commercial comparison page can connect the reader to a relevant merchant or product.

## Internal links

A broad article can link to a deeper guide, which can link to a comparison page.

## Measure each stage

Look at scroll depth, internal clicks, commercial-section views, affiliate CTA clicks and eventual sales.

## Bottom line

A useful funnel is an information sequence, not a trick. Each step should make the next decision easier.
