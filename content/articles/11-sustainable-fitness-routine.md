A sustainable fitness routine is one you can repeat for months.

## Choose a realistic schedule

Build around your actual week rather than an ideal schedule. Consistency is easier when the routine survives busy days.

## Make starting easy

Prepare clothing, choose a regular time and keep equipment accessible. Reduce the number of decisions required to begin.

## Track behaviors

Useful signals include sessions completed, walking time, strength sessions and perceived effort.

For general adults, CDC guidance recommends regular aerobic activity plus muscle-strengthening activity, while individual needs can differ.

## Protect your boundaries

Pain, illness or other health issues may require professional advice. Do not treat a general article as individualized medical guidance.

## Bottom line

Start at an appropriate level, repeat the routine and adjust gradually.
