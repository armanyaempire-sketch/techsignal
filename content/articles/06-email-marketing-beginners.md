Email marketing is a direct communication channel with people who chose to receive messages.

## Start with a clear promise

A subscriber should know what they will receive: a weekly roundup, practical checklist, templates or another useful resource.

## Segment later

As the audience grows, behavior can help define groups such as new subscribers, returning readers, topic-specific readers and customers.

## Automate repetitive steps

A welcome sequence is a common starting point. Automation can also support notifications, reminders and reporting. The objective is not to send more email merely because software makes it easy.

## Measure the whole system

Track delivery, clicks, unsubscribes and the final conversion that matters to the business. For affiliate publishers, outbound commercial clicks should be measured separately.

## Bottom line

A good email program has a clear promise, useful messages, sensible frequency and measurable outcomes.
