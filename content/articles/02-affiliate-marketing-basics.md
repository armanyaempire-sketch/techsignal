Affiliate marketing connects useful publishing with a tracked commercial referral.

## The basic model

A publisher creates content that helps a reader solve a problem. When a relevant commercial option is discussed, the publisher can use a tracked affiliate link. If the required action occurs, the publisher may receive a commission.

## Why content quality matters

A page that only says "buy this" gives the visitor little reason to trust it.

A stronger article explains:

- what problem the product addresses,
- who it is for,
- what should be compared,
- what limitations matter,
- what the buyer should verify.

## Build a funnel

A practical content path is:

Discovery article → deeper guide → comparison → merchant landing page.

The commercial step should match reader intent. Do not force unrelated offers into informational pages.

## Measure the funnel

Track organic visits, internal clicks, affiliate CTA clicks, outbound hops and sales.

## Bottom line

Affiliate marketing works best when the publisher earns trust before asking for the commercial click.
