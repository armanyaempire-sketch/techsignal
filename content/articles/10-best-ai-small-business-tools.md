Small businesses rarely need every AI product. They usually need one product that removes a specific bottleneck.

## Start with the bottleneck

Identify the task that consumes too much time: content drafting, support, notes, research, reporting or follow-up.

## Evaluate the workflow

Check setup effort, integrations, export options, support, usage limits and contract terms.

## Calculate real cost

Subscription price is only part of the cost. Include setup time and the human time needed to review output.

## Think about data

Do not put sensitive information into an AI service without checking the product's privacy and security controls.

## Bottom line

Choose the tool that removes a defined workflow problem with the least friction. Confirm current pricing and terms before purchasing.
