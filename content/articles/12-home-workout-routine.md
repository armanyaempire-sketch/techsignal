Home workouts can be practical when the plan matches your starting point and available equipment.

## Use movement patterns

A simple routine can include a squat or sit-to-stand, hinge, push, pull, carry and core-stability movement. The exact exercise depends on experience, mobility and equipment.

## Plan the week

A beginner can start with a small number of repeatable sessions and regular lighter movement rather than a complicated calendar.

## Measure progression

Progress can mean better technique, an added repetition, slightly more resistance or the same workout becoming easier.

If significant pain or a health issue affects exercise, seek appropriate professional guidance.

## Bottom line

A good home routine is simple enough to repeat and flexible enough to adjust.
