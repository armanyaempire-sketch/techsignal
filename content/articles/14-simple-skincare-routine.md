A beginner skincare routine does not need ten products.

## Start with basics

A simple routine often includes gentle cleansing, moisturizing appropriate for your skin and daytime sun protection.

The American Academy of Dermatology provides everyday skincare guidance for consumers.

## Add one change at a time

If you change everything simultaneously, it becomes difficult to tell what helped or irritated your skin.

## Read labels

Look at ingredients, directions and warnings. Be cautious about dramatic claims that are not supported by reliable information.

## Budget matters

A simple routine that you can maintain may be more useful than a complicated premium routine you stop using.

## Bottom line

Keep the foundation simple and add targeted products only when you know why they belong in your routine.
