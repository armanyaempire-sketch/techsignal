An AI content workflow should begin with a human-defined brief.

## Step 1: Define intent

Write the actual reader question, target audience, primary keyword, secondary questions and any product relevance.

## Step 2: Research first

Collect reliable sources before drafting. Save important pricing, product and health claims so they can be checked later.

## Step 3: Outline

Use AI to suggest structures and missing questions, then edit the outline to match the site's editorial strategy.

## Step 4: Draft and review

Generate sections, mark facts that need verification, remove invented numbers and add original comparisons or examples.

## Step 5: Maintain

Search Console queries and reader behavior can reveal what the article should explain better after publication.

## Bottom line

AI should make the editorial process faster, not replace editorial responsibility.
