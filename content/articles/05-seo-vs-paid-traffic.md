SEO and paid traffic can both bring visitors, but they behave differently.

## SEO

A useful page can continue receiving search impressions after publication. The tradeoff is time: competitive queries can take months to mature.

## Paid traffic

Paid campaigns can create exposure faster, but traffic stops when the budget stops. Campaigns require careful cost and conversion measurement.

## Intent matters

High-intent searches are valuable regardless of channel. For an affiliate publication, phrases such as best, review, alternative, pricing and versus often signal stronger commercial investigation.

## A practical mix

Use SEO to build durable information assets. Use paid traffic selectively when you have a tested offer and a reason to buy traffic.

## Bottom line

SEO is an investment in discoverability. Paid traffic is rented exposure. The right mix depends on economics, budget and time horizon.
