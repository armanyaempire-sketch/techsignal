Buying beauty products online is easier when the comparison is broader than the marketing headline.

## Start with use case

Define what the product is supposed to do and who the intended user is.

## Check the label

Compare ingredients, directions, warnings and actual product information with the seller's marketing copy.

## Compare total cost

Look at price, subscription terms, shipping, package size and cancellation rules.

## Treat strong claims carefully

Promises of instant, guaranteed or universal results deserve extra scrutiny. Distinguish between what the seller claims and what reliable sources support.

## Check seller terms

Confirm current price, return policy and delivery details on the seller's site before purchasing.
