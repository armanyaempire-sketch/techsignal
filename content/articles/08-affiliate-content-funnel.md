The strongest affiliate funnels are educational before they are commercial.

## Build three layers

Discovery pages answer broad questions. Evaluation pages explain how to compare options. Decision pages help a reader choose between actual solutions.

## Match the CTA

An informational page may not need an affiliate CTA. A comparison page usually does. This protects trust and makes commercial pages easier to optimize.

## Track the path

Measure which articles generate qualified clicks, which CTA positions perform and which offers produce sales. Do not force the same product into unrelated pages.

## Bottom line

The affiliate link should be the logical next step after the content has helped the reader understand the problem and the available solutions.
